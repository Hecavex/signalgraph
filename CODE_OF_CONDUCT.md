# Code of Conduct

SignalGraph's community is for constructive, lawful defensive-security collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy, authorization boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Keep examples safe and remove credentials, personal data, private intelligence, and customer information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about safety, scope, licensing, and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publication of secrets, unsafe exploit instructions, unauthorized targeting, or attempts to use the project for malicious activity are not permitted.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect people, the project, or the community. Report sensitive security matters through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/Hecavex/signalgraph/security/advisories/new). For other private conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through [Hecavex](https://hecavex.com) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/deilis).

By participating, you agree to follow these standards in repository issues, discussions, pull requests, and other SignalGraph community spaces.
